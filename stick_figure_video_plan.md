## STORY + NARRATION (8 scenes)

1. HOOK – "Twenty thousand years ago, a boy named Oko woke at midnight, and two glowing eyes were staring right back at him."
2. THE QUESTION – "Why were people like Oko so afraid of the dark? The answer is not what you think."
3. THE PROBLEM – "One evening, Oko's little sister dropped her favorite toy by the river, and the sun was sinking fast."
4. SOMETHING GOES WRONG – "Darkness fell. Oko could not see his own hands, and something big was moving in the grass."
5. THE REASON – "Humans see badly at night. But lions and leopards hunt best in the dark, and they see very well."
6. THE ESCAPE – "Far away, Oko saw a tiny orange glow. Fire! He ran toward it, and the beasts stayed back."
7. THE ELDER – "Grandmother smiled. 'Fear is a gift,' she said. 'It kept our family alive for a very long time.'"
8. SURPRISING ANSWER – "The ones who feared the dark are your ancestors. And those glowing eyes? Just a tiny owl, wondering who woke it up."
